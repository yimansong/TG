Governance Team

Ensuring Responsible, Strategic, and Effective AI at Loudoun Water

Governance Team: Mission

To oversee all AI initiatives at Loudoun Water, protecting ethical integrity, compliance, and organizational alignment while managing risks and ensuring each project delivers measurable value.

Core Responsibilities

1. Define AI Governance Frameworks:
  • Develop and enforce policies on ethics, data privacy, and regulatory compliance.
  • Set security and model management standards for all AI initiatives.
2. Strategic Alignment & Oversight:
  • Ensure all AI projects align with business priorities and mission.
  • Approve and prioritize new initiatives based on clear criteria.
3. Risk Management:
  • Assess and address risks such as bias, transparency, cybersecurity, and data misuse.
4. Model & Data Lifecycle Management:
  • Oversee model development, deployment, monitoring, and retirement.
  • Ensure robust data management and auditability.
5. Ethics & Compliance:
  • Monitor and enforce ethical AI practices and compliance with regulations.
6. Continuous Improvement:
  • Measure project outcomes, collect feedback, and report performance to leadership.
  • Drive ongoing improvement of AI practices.

Key Activities

  • Standardizing AI processes and best practices.
  • Approving, evaluating, and tracking AI initiatives.
  • Fostering knowledge-sharing and collaboration across business and technical teams.
  • Conducting audits and reviews for adherence and transparency.

Our Value

  • Safeguards trust and transparency in all AI solutions.
  • Aligns AI with Loudoun Water’s mission and customer needs.
  • Reduces risk, ensures compliance, and promotes ethical leadership in AI.
  • Enables innovative, efficient, and responsible use of advanced technologies.
