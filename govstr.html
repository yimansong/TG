Pillar I: People

AI CoE Governance Team

The AI CoE Governance Team provides oversight of Loudoun Water’s AI program.

Project Sponsor

The Project Sponsor is responsible for business justification and operational impact.

Business Owner

The Business Owner ensures the integrity and quality of datasets used for model training, provide human oversight for any AI-assisted decisions.

Technical Lead

The Technical Lead is responsible for implementation integrity and smooth transition to operations.

Pillar II: Policies

Acceptable Use (AUP)

Guidelines for public LLMs (no PII/trade secrets) and prohibited use cases.

Data Governance & Privacy

Protocols for anonymization and encryption for AI training sets.

Technical & Security Standard

Technical and security standards for implementing and integrating AI.

Vendor Management

Third-party vetting and updated NDAs protecting proprietary training.

Risk Management

Lifecycle risk management.

Cloud Solution Management

Guidance and process of using cloud based AI solutions.
