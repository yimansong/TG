Pillar I: People

Oversight & Strategic Leadership

AI CoE Governance Team

  • The AI CoE Governance Team provides oversight of Loudoun Water’s AI program. AI governance is cross-functional. Representation may include Technology Services, Operations, Engineering, Human Resources, and other departments as appropriate to the use case.

Operational Compliance

Project Sponsor & Solution Owner

  • Each AI initiative must have a clearly identified Project Sponsor responsible for business justification and operational impact, and a Solution owner for business-level compliance responsibility.

Execution & Support

Technical Lead

  • The technical Lead is responsible for implementation integrity and smooth transition to operations.

Pillar II: Policies

Acceptable Use (AUP)

Guidelines for public LLMs (no PII/trade secrets) and prohibited use cases.

Data Governance & Privacy

Protocols for anonymization and encryption for AI training sets.

Vendor Management

Third-party vetting and updated NDAs protecting proprietary training.

Risk Management

Lifecycle risk management.
