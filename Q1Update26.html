Multimodal Leap (Amber)

2025 marked the move to Native Multimodality. Benchmarks like Video-MME now test temporal reasoning—understanding why an event happened over a 60-minute window, rather than just describing a static frame.

Closed-Loop Coding

Coding success (80.9%) is driven by Self-Correction. The model acts as an agent that can compile, test, and iterate until the benchmark passes, which is why it far outpaces open-world agency.

The Reliability Gap

General Agency (Terminal-Bench) lacks a clear "pass/fail" sensor. A single mistake in a system admin task can lead to Spaghetti Divergence, explaining the current ~57% plateau.
