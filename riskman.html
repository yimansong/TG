Our Guiding Principles

Every AI initiative at Loudoun Water is built on a foundation of core values that ensure we act in the best interest of the public.

Public Trust & Transparency

We ensure our AI systems are fair, accountable, and explainable to build and maintain your trust.

Safety & Reliability

The integrity of our critical infrastructure is paramount. Our AI systems are rigorously tested to ensure they enhance, not compromise, safety.

Security & Privacy

We protect your data. Our AI systems are built with robust security controls to safeguard sensitive information.

Equity & Fairness

We actively work to ensure our AI systems are fair, equitable, and do not perpetuate or amplify existing biases.

Human-in-the-Loop

Technology supports, but does not replace, human expertise. Critical decisions will always be subject to human review and approval.

Our Framework in Action

The first step is understanding potential risks across the AI lifecycle. This includes:

  • Data Quality and Bias: Ensuring our data is accurate, complete, and doesn't lead to flawed AI models.
  • Model Performance and Reliability: Making sure our models perform as expected and don't cause operational issues.
  • Cybersecurity: Protecting our AI systems from attacks like data poisoning or model tampering.
  • Ethical and Social Impacts: Considering unintended consequences like algorithmic bias.

We implement specific strategies to manage identified risks, including:

  • Data Governance: Implementing robust practices to ensure data quality, integrity, and security.
  • Model Validation and Testing: Rigorously testing models for accuracy, reliability, and fairness before deployment.
  • Cybersecurity Controls: Using strong security measures to protect our AI systems from threats.
  • Ethical Review: Establishing a review process to assess the social and ethical impacts of AI projects.
  • Human Oversight: Ensuring meaningful human supervision for all high-risk AI applications.

Clear roles and responsibilities are essential for effective risk management:

AI Steering Committee

A cross-functional team providing strategic direction and oversight for our entire AI program.

Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Leads the implementation of this framework and ensures AI initiatives align with our core mission.

Data Scientists & Engineers

Manage the day-to-day development and deployment of AI models in accordance with this framework.

We manage risks at every stage of an AI model's life, from concept to retirement.

  1. 1. Conception & Design

    Define the problem and assess the feasibility and risks of using AI.

  2. 2. Data Collection & Preparation

    Ensure data is accurate, complete, and representative.

  3. 3. Model Development & Training

    Build the model and test for bias and fairness.

  4. 4. Model Validation & Testing

    Rigorously test the model's performance and reliability.

  5. 5. Deployment

    Release the model with proper monitoring and controls.

  6. 6. Monitoring & Maintenance

    Continuously monitor performance and retrain as needed.

  7. 7. Retirement

    Decommission the model when it's no longer effective or needed.

A Living Document

This framework is not static. We will continuously review and update our approach as AI technologies and best practices evolve, ensuring we always operate at the forefront of responsible innovation.
