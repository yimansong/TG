Our Guiding Principles

Human Oversight

AI is a tool to assist our expert staff, not replace their judgment. Critical decisions are always made or reviewed by our qualified team members.

Fairness & Equity

We design our systems to be fair, ensuring our services and operations benefit everyone in our community without bias.

Security & Reliability

Protecting our infrastructure and your data is a top priority. We rigorously test our technology to ensure it performs reliably and safely.

Transparency

We believe in being open about how we operate. When you interact with an AI-powered tool, like a customer service chatbot, we will let you know.

Accountability

We take full responsibility for the technology we use and have established clear internal oversight to review and guide our AI initiatives.

Privacy Protection

We are stewards of your data. Our use of AI follows strict privacy standards and all applicable data protection laws to safeguard your information.
